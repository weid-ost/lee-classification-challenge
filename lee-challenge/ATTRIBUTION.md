# Image and methodology attribution

## Prototype image assets

1. **Leading edge damage - windturbine.jpg** — Belzona Polymerics Ltd.; hosted on Wikimedia Commons; **CC0 1.0**. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Leading_edge_damage_-_windturbine.jpg
2. **LEEv1.4 dataset sample images** — Dubai International Academy, via Roboflow Universe; **CC BY 4.0**. Source: https://universe.roboflow.com/dubai-international-academy/leev1.4

The prototype intentionally creates 30 assessment *views* from these four public source images using different crops/zoom positions. This is for UI testing only. A scientific challenge should replace them with 30 independent, rights-cleared inspection images spanning the intended severity range.

## Classification systems

- LERCat, Table 2 in *A categorisation scheme for energy losses and noise increase due to leading edge roughness*, Journal of Physics: Conference Series 3232 (2026) 012012: https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1742-6596/3232/1/012012

The interface paraphrases/condenses category labels for usability. Verify wording against the source publications before a formal study launch.
